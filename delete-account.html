Delete your Roobai account

Last updated: 16 October 2026

You can delete your Roobai account and all the data in it yourself, or ask us to do it.

In the app

  1. Open Roobai and tap your profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the note and tap Delete my account.

Your data is removed from our live systems straight away, and you're signed out.

If you can't open the app

Email balajipari07@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Roobai account". We'll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your transactions, bills and patterns.
  • Your budget, labels and chats with Pat.
  • Your sign-in details and the saved connection to your Gmail.
  • Any text of payment alerts we still hold.

This can't be undone.

Your Gmail access

When you delete your account we remove Roobai's access to your Gmail. You can also remove it yourself at any time in your Google Account's security settings.

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and how long we keep it.
