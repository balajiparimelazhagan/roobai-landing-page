Most money apps are good at showing what you already spent. The worry is about what's next. The bill you forgot, the EMI you half-remember, the week money runs thin.

"I still write down every expense, just to guess what next month will cost."

"My bank balance says I'm fine. It never mentions the ₹22,000 rent due Friday."

"One month feels under control. The next one falls apart again."

"I keep meaning to start an emergency fund. The budget's too messy to begin."

BUDGET

A budget that lasts past the first week

Most budgets die the same way. You build one in a spreadsheet, the first odd bill throws it off, and a week later you've stopped looking. Roobai's budget starts from the bills you've already okayed, stays grouped the way your money actually moves, and shows when a category is drifting while you can still do something about it.

Seeded, not started from scratch
Every commitment you approved on Patterns lands here automatically, already grouped and dated.
Grouped the way money actually moves
Income, Bills & EMIs, Savings & Investments and Flexible Spending each get their own group, not one long list.
Drift shows up before month-end
Categories like groceries show spent-so-far against your usual, as the month goes, not in a report afterwards.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions people ask us

What is Roobai?

Roobai is a money app for people in India who want to know if the month is under control. It shows what you've spent, what bills are still coming, and how much is safe to spend right now.

How is it different from a budgeting app?

Budgeting apps ask you to set limits and stick to them every month. Roobai drafts a budget from the bills you already pay, then shows what's realistically safe to spend as the month goes. There are no rules to keep up.

Do I have to enter my expenses?

No. Roobai fills in your spending for you, so there's no logging every auto ride and cup of chai. You just check that it got things right and fix anything that's off.

How does Roobai see my transactions?

Roobai reads the payment updates you already get on your phone. There's no net-banking password, ever. Nothing leaves your account, and Roobai never moves money for you.

Is my data safe?

Your information stays yours. It's encrypted, you can disconnect any time, and Roobai doesn't sell data or run ads.

What does "safe to spend" mean?

It's what's left after your expected income minus rent, EMIs, bills and a small buffer for surprises. It isn't your bank balance and it isn't a strict budget. It's the figure that tells you how much you can spend without the month going off track.

Who is Pat?

Pat is the assistant inside Roobai. Ask it something like "can I afford this?" and it answers from your own numbers, in plain language. Pat gives guidance to think with, not licensed financial advice, and it doesn't pick stocks.
