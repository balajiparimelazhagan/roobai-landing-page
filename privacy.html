Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Your money is personal, so we keep this simple. Here's what Roobai collects, why, and how we look after it. Questions? Write to us at balajipari07@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in: name, email and photo.
  • Payment alerts from the Gmail inbox and SMS you connect. We only look at messages that match bank and payment-alert wording. From each one we pick out the amount, date, payee, last digits of the account and reference number. We delete the text of each alert as soon as we've read it, or within two days if we couldn't read it. The details we picked out, plus the sender and date, stay in your account until you delete it.
  • What you do in Roobai: labels, bills you confirm, your budget and your chats with Pat.

Why we need it

  • To show your spending and upcoming bills.
  • To tell you what's safe to spend.
  • To answer your questions in Pat.

That's it. We don't use it for anything else.

Our promises

  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • We don't ask for your net-banking password.
  • Roobai never moves your money.
  • We only read your Gmail. We never send, delete or change anything.
  • Nobody at Roobai reads your messages, unless you ask us to fix a problem or the law or security needs it.

Gmail and Google data

We use Google data only for the features you see in Roobai. Gmail access is read-only, and we use it only to find your payment alerts.

We don't use your Google data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models, ours or anyone else's. Nobody gets your Gmail data for advertising.

Roobai's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Alert emails and SMS text: deleted as soon as we've read them, or within two days if we couldn't.
  • The details we picked out, your bills, budget, labels and chats: until you delete your account.
  • Sync history: 30 days. Notifications: 90 days.
  • Your Gmail connection: until you remove access or delete your account.

Pat and AI

For now, Pat uses OpenAI, a third-party AI service, to put its answers into plain words, to sort payees into categories and to spot bills that repeat. Roobai works out your numbers itself. OpenAI only gets what it needs: your question and the figures to answer it, and, for sorting, a payee's name with the dates and amounts of its payments. It never gets the text, sender or subject of your emails or SMS, and we don't send your name or email address. Under OpenAI's API terms, it doesn't use this data to train its models.

Who else is involved

  • Google: sign-in, Gmail and fonts.
  • OpenAI: for Pat, as above.
  • InterServer: our servers, in Secaucus, New Jersey, USA.

We share your data with authorities only if the law requires it.

How we keep it safe

  • Your data is encrypted in transit, and the token that lets us read your Gmail is encrypted before we store it.
  • Everyone's records are kept separate at the database level.
  • We never see your net-banking password, because we never ask for it.

You're in control

  • Stop Gmail access any time in your Google Account's security settings. Stop SMS access in your phone's app settings.
  • Delete your account and data in the app: open your profile and tap Delete account. We delete your transactions, bills, budget, labels, chats and sign-in details, and we remove Roobai's access to your Gmail. This can't be undone. You can also ask us to do it: see how to delete your account.
  • Want a copy of your data or a correction? Email us and we'll take care of it.
  • You can also name someone to act for you.

Need help?

Write to Balaji Parimelazhagan at balajipari07@gmail.com. He looks after privacy questions and complaints.

Good to know

  • Roobai is for people aged 18 and over.
  • If we change this page, we'll update the date and tell you about big changes. We'll ask you again before using your data in a new way.
